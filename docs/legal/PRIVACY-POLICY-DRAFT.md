# ErrandCONNECT Privacy Policy (DRAFT – for legal review, not yet in force)

_Last updated: [date]_

ErrandCONNECT ("we") connects people who need errands done with runners who do them, in Kenya. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** name, phone number, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), security PIN (hashed), referral code.
- **Runners:** national ID or passport details and photos, selfie, KRA PIN, vehicle details and documents, next of kin, payout details, background check answers.
- **Errands:** pickup and drop-off addresses, items and descriptions, recipient name and phone, chat messages, ratings and reviews.
- **Location:** customers' saved addresses; runners' live location while online and while doing an errand.
- **Payments:** M-Pesa phone numbers and receipts, wallet transactions, withdrawals. We never see your M-Pesa PIN.
- **Device and security:** device name, IP address and sign-in times, used to keep accounts secure and prevent fraud.

## Why we use it (lawful basis)

- To provide the service: match errands with runners, show progress, take and pass on payments (contract).
- To keep the platform safe: verify runners, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes (legitimate interests; legal obligations).
- To meet legal duties: tax and financial record keeping.
- To send offers and rewards, only where you have agreed (consent; you can opt out).

## Who we share it with

- The other party to your errand: first name, photo, rating, and phone number only while the errand is in progress.
- Safaricom (M-Pesa), SMS providers and hosting providers, under contract, only as needed.
- Authorities where the law requires it.
  We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

Account data while your account is open. When you delete your account we erase your personal details, documents and payout details; records of errands and payments are kept without your personal details for as long as tax and financial laws require (currently up to [7] years).

## Your rights

You can access your data (Profile → Privacy & account → Download my data), correct it, object to or restrict some uses, withdraw consent, and delete your account in the app. You can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

## Security

Encryption in transit, hashed passwords and PINs, two-factor sign-in for staff, role-based access and audit logs.
